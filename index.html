Print the sheet
Position 1 is the top-left label. Bump it up if you're reusing a partly-peeled sheet. The nudge fields shift everything a hair if your printer runs off-centre.
In the print dialog set Scale: 100% (or "Actual size") and Margins: None, or the labels will drift off the sticker grid.
